Your Store Data, Synced Automatically.

Approved & Published on the BigCommerce App Marketplace

SyncBig pushes your BigCommerce orders, products, and customers straight into Google Sheets, Asana, and Slack — on a schedule you define, with no code and no manual exports.

Get Started → Read The User Guide

Running a BigCommerce store generates a constant stream of valuable data — new orders, updated products, changing customer records — but getting that data into the tools your team actually works in has always meant manual exports, expensive integrations, or custom development.

SyncBig closes that gap.

SyncBig is a native BigCommerce app that automatically pushes your store data to Google Sheets, Asana, and Slack on a schedule you define. Whether your operations team needs a live spreadsheet of today's orders, your product team wants Asana tasks created for every new SKU, or your sales team wants a Slack alert whenever new customers sign up — SyncBig handles it automatically, in the background, without any code.

What You Can Sync

Three Data Types, Fully Configurable

Choose the BigCommerce records you want to track — SyncBig keeps every field mapping under your control.

Orders

Orders

Statuses, totals, customer details, line items, and more — every order event captured as it happens.

Products

Products

SKUs, names, prices, inventory levels, and more — your full catalogue kept current everywhere it needs to be.

Customers

Customers

IDs, names, email addresses, and more — flowing to sales and marketing the moment they sign up.

Where You Can Send It

Three Destinations, Zero Glue Code

Point SyncBig at the tool your team already lives in — it handles the formatting and delivery.

Google Sheets

Google Sheets

Data lands directly in a spreadsheet you control, formatted with a header row and one row per record.

Asana

Asana

Records become tasks in a project you choose, with custom fields mapped to the data you select.

Slack

Slack

Records are posted as structured messages or a single summary alert to any channel.

How Scheduling Works

Set It Once. It Stays Current.

Choose a cadence and SyncBig takes care of the rest — including keeping destinations free of duplicates.

Hourly Every 6 Hours Every 12 Hours Daily Weekly Monthly One-Time Run

Incremental by default. All schedules run in UTC and SyncBig handles incremental syncing automatically — after the first full sync, each subsequent run only fetches records that have changed since the last run, keeping your destinations up to date without duplicating data.

Full Visibility

Every Sync, Fully Audited

Know exactly what ran, when, and whether it succeeded — at a glance or in detail.

Sync History Log

A complete audit trail of every sync — when it ran, how many records were synced, and whether it succeeded or failed.

Live Dashboard

See active jobs, syncs completed today, and any failures at a glance, without digging through logs.
