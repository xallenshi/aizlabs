Questions

The Things Merchants Actually Ask

Do I need to upload my photos anywhere?

No. StyleBig reads your BigCommerce catalogue directly — you browse categories, pick products, and generate. Results save straight back onto the product. There is no export, no re-upload, and no separate library to keep in sync.

What happens to my product images?

They are sent to OpenAI for processing and the generated result is stored so it can appear in your history. Your images are never used to train a model and are never shared with other merchants.

What counts as a credit?

One generated image, whichever tool made it. There are no quality tiers and no generations that cost more than others. A generation that fails is refunded automatically — you are never charged for an image you did not get.

Do unused credits roll over?

Monthly credits reset at the start of each billing cycle, so a plan is a monthly allowance rather than a balance that accumulates. If you consistently run out before the month is up, the next plan up is the answer — you can upgrade at any time and the new allowance applies immediately.

Why was my first charge smaller than the plan price?

BigCommerce aligns an app subscription to your store's own billing date and charges only for the days remaining in that period. Your credits for that first partial period are prorated to match, and the full monthly allowance arrives on the next renewal.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your BigCommerce control panel. Billing runs through BigCommerce itself, so there is no separate account or card on file with us, and credits you have already been granted stay usable.
