A BigCommerce App by AI Zone Labs

Product Photos, Reimagined by AI.

StyleBig turns a single plain product photo into lifestyle scenes, holiday-themed visuals, and model showcases — right inside your BigCommerce admin, with no photographer, no design software, and no code.

See How to Install → Read The User Guide

Great product photography sells — but a photo shoot for every scene, season, and use case is slow and expensive, and most merchants ship with whatever plain catalogue shot they started with.

StyleBig closes that gap.

StyleBig is a native BigCommerce app that generates new, on-brand imagery using AI — lifestyle scenes, seasonal overlays, and model showcases built from your existing product photos, precision tools to erase or extend any image, and a Freestyle mode for standalone assets that need no product photo at all. Review each result, then push it straight into your product's image gallery.

What You Can Create

Four Studio Tools, Endless New Imagery

Pick a product from your catalogue and generate new imagery around it — or skip the catalogue entirely and create a standalone asset from scratch.

Scene Studio

Scene Studio

Place a product into a styled lifestyle scene — describe the setting, or start from a preset, and StyleBig builds the backdrop around it.

Holiday Overlay

Holiday Overlay

Drop a product into a seasonal or holiday-themed visual — built for campaign creative that changes with the calendar, without a new shoot each time.

Model Showcase

Model Showcase

Show wearable or handheld products on an AI-generated model — a fast way to give a flat-lay product some real context and scale.

Freestyle

Freestyle — Create Any Image

No product needed — write your own prompt, start from a preset, or hit Surprise Me to generate a model turnaround, backdrop, or promo graphic from scratch.

Precision Editing

Fix Or Extend Any Image, By Hand

Not every edit needs a whole new scene — sometimes you just need to remove something, or give a tightly-cropped photo more room to breathe.

Inpaint

Inpaint — Erase & Regenerate

Brush over the part of a photo you want gone — a stray reflection, a background object, an old logo — and StyleBig regenerates just that area, seamlessly.

Outpaint

Outpaint — Expand The Canvas

Extend a tightly-cropped photo outward — turn a square product shot into a wide banner or tall hero image without losing the original.

How Credits Work

Priced By Quality, Not By Tool

Every generation costs credits based on the quality level you choose — every studio tool and resolution uses the same simple scale.

Low Quality — 1 credit Medium Quality — 1 credit High Quality — 3 credits

Every plan includes every tool. Scene Studio, Holiday Overlay, Model Showcase, Freestyle, Inpaint, and Outpaint are all available on every plan — the only thing that changes between plans is how many credits you get each month.
