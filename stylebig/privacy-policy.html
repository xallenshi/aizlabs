AI Zone Labs (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the StyleBig application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you install and use the App through the BigCommerce platform.

Information We Collect

When you install and use StyleBig, we collect and store the following information:

  • BigCommerce store data: Your store hash, OAuth access token, and the OAuth scopes granted during installation (view and modify products, and view and modify general store information and settings). This is required to read your product catalogue and write generated images back to it.
  • BigCommerce user data: The user ID, email address, and username of BigCommerce users who install or access the App.
  • Generation requests: The studio tool, prompt or edit instructions, quality setting, and resolution you select for each image generation, and the product it's tied to when applicable (Freestyle generations are not tied to a product).
  • Generation history: A log of each generation — timestamp, product association, credits charged, and a reference to the resulting image.
  • Billing and subscription data: Your plan tier, credit balance, and billing history. Payment itself is processed by BigCommerce's own billing system — we do not collect or store your card details.

Generated Images

Source product photos and AI-generated results are processed to fulfill your generation requests and are stored so you can review and reuse them from the Generations page. An image is only written to your BigCommerce product's public image gallery when you choose to push it there.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Authenticate your store and read your product catalogue on your behalf
  • Generate images using the studio tool and settings you select, and write results to your store when you request it
  • Track and enforce your credit balance and plan entitlements
  • Display generation history, plan status, and billing history within the App
  • Diagnose and resolve technical errors

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Your store data (tokens, generation history, and billing records) is retained for as long as the App is installed on your store. When you uninstall the App from BigCommerce, your OAuth access is revoked and associated store data is deleted from our systems, subject to what we're required to retain for billing and legal record-keeping.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including encrypted database connections (TLS), environment-variable-based secret management, and scoped OAuth tokens.

Third-Party Services

StyleBig relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • OpenAI API (OpenAI, L.L.C.) — source photos and prompts are sent to OpenAI's image generation API to produce each result.
  • BigCommerce Unified Billing — processes your subscription payment; we never see or store your card details.

Your use of these services is governed by their respective privacy policies.
